Tiny Trails

a cozy bug-spotting adventure

Guide Lottie the ladybug explorer across the forest floor. Sneak up on 17 tiny creatures and snap their photo for your journal. Some only come out at night…

How to play

🐞 Move: arrow keys / WASD, or drag anywhere on mobile.
🤫 Sneak: hold Shift (or drag just a little). Rush in and bugs get spooked ! and flee.
📸 Photograph: stay close and still-ish until the ring fills.
💧 Dewdrops: collect them to buy hats for Lottie.
🕷️ Spiders hunt in the dark woods and the old log.
🐦 A bird shadow grows above you: run, or hide under a clover or mushroom!
🌧️ Rain leaves puddles that slow you down.
🌙 Night falls halfway: fireflies, moths and crickets appear.
⭐ Rarity: common · rare · legendary
