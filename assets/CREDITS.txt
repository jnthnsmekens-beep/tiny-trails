Tiny Trails – asset credits & licenses
=======================================
Original (made for this project, by Grok Bot for Jonathan Smekens):
- theme-preview.mp3 / theme loop: original composition "Tiny Trails Theme", rendered with synthesized
  music box, kalimba and Karplus-Strong plucked guitar (numpy). No existing melodies used.
- discovery-jingle.mp3: original, reuses the theme's opening motif.
- ground.jpg: procedurally painted moss texture (numpy/PIL).
- All sprites (Lottie, insects, decorations, UI) are drawn in code on <canvas>.
- UI pops, dew tinkles, camera shutter, owl hoot, bee buzz, hit thump: synthesized with the Web Audio API.

Recorded sounds (all CC0 / public domain, no attribution required; credited anyway):
- birds.mp3 – "Ambient Bird Sounds" by isaiah658, CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/ambient-bird-sounds
- crickets.mp3 – "Crickets Ambient Noise - loopable" by Wolfgang_ (Ted Kerr), CC0
  https://opengameart.org/content/crickets-ambient-noise-loopable
- rain.mp3 – "AMB Rain Loop 1" by Kresiek The Furry, CC0 (trimmed to 40 s, low-passed)
  https://opengameart.org/content/amb-rain-loop-1
- step1-3.mp3 – from "Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)" by Fantozzi (submitted by qubodup), CC0
  (pitched up and high-passed to sound like tiny leaf-litter steps)
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
Edits: converted to mono MP3, filtered/trimmed with ffmpeg.

v6 additions (assets/sfx/, all CC0, pitched/filtered/trimmed with ffmpeg):

- sand1-3, stone1-2, cave1-2: Fantozzi's Footsteps (Sand & Stone), CC0 – https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone (cave = added echo)
- leaves1-3, rustle: Kenney "RPG Audio" (footstep01/04/07) and Impact Sounds (footstep_grass_001), CC0 – https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
